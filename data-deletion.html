Data Deletion Instructions

Marketing Research Engine

This page explains how you can request the deletion of information associated with Marketing Research Engine.

How to request data deletion

Any user may request the deletion of their personal information processed by this application at any time, free of charge.

To submit a request:

  1. Send an email to faiscacppa@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the Instagram username or other information that will help identify the relevant data.
  4. Specify that you are requesting deletion of your data associated with Marketing Research Engine.

You do not need to provide your Instagram password or access token.

What happens next?

After receiving a request, the developer will identify the relevant information processed by the application and delete it, unless retention is required by applicable law.

A confirmation will be sent to the email address used to submit the request.

Revoking Instagram access

You may also revoke previously granted permissions through your Meta or Instagram account settings. Revoking access does not replace a request to delete information already stored by the application.
