Privacy Policy

Marketing Research Engine

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. About the Application

Marketing Research Engine is an independent experimental project developed for educational, research and personal use. Its purpose is to explore automated monitoring of marketing trends and publicly available brand communication on social media platforms, primarily Instagram.

The application is currently a small-scale prototype operated by a single individual. It is not a publicly available service and is not intended for use by multiple users.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the functionality being tested and permissions granted through Meta, the application may access information made available through the official Instagram API. This may include account information, public content and information about published posts.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Information is used exclusively for educational and experimental purposes, including monitoring marketing trends, researching brand communication and testing automated data collection and analytical processes.

4. Data Sharing

The application is operated by one individual and is not intended to distribute collected information to other users or sell it to third parties. Any external service providers used to operate the application may process data as necessary for their services.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Information is stored only to the extent necessary for the project's research and testing activities. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the functionality being tested.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the application.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with the application by contacting the developer at: faiscacppa@gmail.com.

8. Third-Party Services

The application uses Meta's developer platform and Instagram API. Their use is subject to Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies.
